## Initial Seed Content (add these)

### 1) Wet Signature
- definition: A physical signature made with pen on a printed document (not a digital signature).
- why: Many offices require paper signatures for verification; digital signatures may be rejected.
- steps:
  1. Download/export the form or document (PDF).
  2. Print it.
  3. Sign with pen (director/authorized signatory).
  4. Apply company stamp if required.
  5. Scan or photograph clearly.
  6. Upload to DSCVR and attach to the relevant compliance item.
- store: Signed PDF scan; photo of signature page if needed.
- pitfalls: Low-quality scans; missing signature page; wrong signatory.

### 2) Company Stamp / Chop
- definition: A company seal/stamp used to authenticate documents.
- why: Often requested to confirm documents are officially issued by the business.
- steps (optional):
  1. Print document.
  2. Apply stamp where indicated (often near signature).
  3. Scan + upload.
- store: Scanned stamped document; photo evidence if required.
- pitfalls: Using the wrong stamp; stamping over key text.

### 3) Notarised Document
- definition: A document that has been verified/authenticated by a notary.
- why: Used to confirm identities/signatures and document authenticity.
- store: Notary-certified copy PDF; receipt/reference number if issued.
- pitfalls: Confusing notarised vs certified copy; using expired/incorrect versions.

### 4) Legalised Copy
- definition: A copy that has been formally verified by an authority/notary as a true copy.
- why: Some applications require legalised copies instead of originals.
- store: Legalised PDF scan; supporting receipt.
- pitfalls: Uploading a normal photocopy instead of legalised copy.

### 5) Certified Copy
- definition: A copy signed/stamped by an authorised person confirming it matches the original.
- why: Often accepted where originals aren’t required.
- store: Certified scan; certifier details.
- pitfalls: Missing certifier details/date.

### 6) OSS (Online Single Submission)
- definition: Indonesia’s online portal used to register businesses and manage certain permits.
- why: Many registrations and licences start here; it’s a central record.
- store: Screenshots of submission confirmation; PDF outputs; registration numbers.
- pitfalls: Losing login credentials; incomplete fields leading to rejections.

### 7) NIB (Business Identification Number)
- definition: A core business identification number issued through OSS.
- why: Often required before other permits, tax registrations, and operational steps.
- store: NIB certificate PDF; OSS confirmation.
- pitfalls: Wrong business category data; mismatched company details.

### 8) KBLI
- definition: Indonesia’s business activity classification codes.
- why: The selected KBLI affects what licences/permits apply.
- store: KBLI selection record; consultant recommendation file (if any).
- pitfalls: Selecting the wrong KBLI and needing rework.

### 9) PBG
- definition: Building approval/permit for construction or building status changes.
- why: Needed for legal building approval and downstream certificates.
- store: PBG approval documents; drawings; inspection notes.
- pitfalls: Missing technical drawings; assuming old permits are still valid.

### 10) SLF
- definition: Certificate that confirms a building is fit for function/occupancy.
- why: Often required for commercial operation and audits; may require renewal.
- store: SLF certificate PDF; inspection reports; renewal reminders.
- pitfalls: Letting it expire; missing inspection evidence.
