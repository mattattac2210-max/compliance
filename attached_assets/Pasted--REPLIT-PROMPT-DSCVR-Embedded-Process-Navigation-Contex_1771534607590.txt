# REPLIT PROMPT — DSCVR: Embedded Process Navigation + Contextual Glossary (FINAL STRUCTURE)

We are building DSCVR’s Compliance Guide system.

This is NOT documentation.

This is operational workflow intelligence embedded directly inside compliance steps.

We need two connected layers:

1️⃣ Process Navigation (primary UX)  
2️⃣ Terminology Decoder (reference database)

Glossary definitions must appear contextually inside workflow steps via hover hyperlinks.

---

## GLOBAL DISCLAIMER (DISPLAY IN GUIDE HEADER)

"This guide provides general workflow patterns and document-handling guidance based on industry practice. Requirements may vary by regency and may change. This is not legal or tax advice. Confirm with a licensed consultant or authority."

# 1️⃣ DATA STRUCTURE

## Table: compliance_terms

Fields:

- id (uuid)
- term (string, unique)
- slug (string, unique)
- plain_definition (text)
- why_it_matters (json array)
- what_to_store (json array)
- common_pitfalls (json array, optional)
- synonyms (json array, optional)
- last_updated (date)
- is_active (boolean)

---

## Table: process_navigation_guides

Fields:

- id (uuid)
- gate_number (int)
- title (string)
- summary (text)
- authority_handled_by (string)
- submission_type (enum: digital | physical | hybrid)
- sequence_steps (json array)
- what_to_expect (json array)
- typical_delays (json array)
- common_rejection_reasons (json array)
- dscvr_recommended_storage (json array)
- last_updated (date)
- is_active (boolean)

---

## sequence_steps object structure

Each step contains:

- step_number
- action_description
- where_it_goes
- digital_or_physical (digital | physical | hybrid)
- who_handles_it
- notes (optional)
- expand_details (json object, optional)

expand_details may include:

- why_this_matters
- common_issues
- preparation_tips
- storage_reminders

# 3️⃣ EMBEDDED GLOSSARY HOVER SYSTEM (CRITICAL)

Glossary is NOT separate from steps.

It is embedded contextually.

---

## Behavior

Any glossary term appearing in:

- action_description
- notes
- expand_details
- what_to_expect
- delays
- rejection reasons

Should automatically render as a hover hyperlink.

---

## Example

Text:

"Apply wet signature and company stamp."

Rendered:

Apply **wet signature** and **company stamp** (hover enabled)

---

## Hover Tooltip Shows

- Term title
- Short definition
- 1 “why it matters” bullet
- Button: “View full guide”

Click opens full glossary entry modal.

---

# 4️⃣ COMPONENTS TO BUILD

## GlossaryLink Component

Props:

- term
- children

Behavior:

- Dotted underline
- Hover popover
- Click opens glossary modal

---

## GlossaryAwareText Renderer

Function:

- Scans text
- Matches glossary terms
- Wraps matches in GlossaryLink

Requirements:

- Case insensitive
- Word boundary matching
- Synonym support
- Longest match priority
- Safe rendering (no XSS)
