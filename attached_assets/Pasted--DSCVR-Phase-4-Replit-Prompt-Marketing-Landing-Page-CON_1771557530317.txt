## THE LANDING PAGE — `client/src/pages/landing.tsx`

### Design philosophy

This is a **dark, editorial, intelligence-grade** marketing page. The audience is sophisticated foreign villa operators — entrepreneurs, not tourists. They understand risk. They are time-poor. They have seen bad advice cost them real money.

The tone is: **authoritative, direct, zero fluff.** Not "we help you succeed" — but "here is exactly what will block your listing on March 31 and here is what DSCVR does about it." Lead with the threat, follow with the solution.

Visual language: Deep navy (#07101E), teal accents (#14B8A6), the same grid overlay and Montserrat/Lato as the app. It should feel like the same product — not a separate marketing site. Think intelligence briefing, not SaaS landing page.

**The key UX mechanic:** The compliance tool content is *teased* — visitors can see the gate structure, blurred/locked content, and a preview of what they're missing — but cannot interact without an account. This creates desire through partial visibility, not just description.

**DSCVR tracks:**
- Licence documents and expiry dates

- Staff documentation and permit status
- Filing calendar deadlines
- Safety compliance task logs
- SOP execution evidence

**DSCVR does not:**
- Interpret zoning or legal status
- Provide legal or tax advice
- Validate corporate structures
- File returns or submissions
- Certify compliance status

## ANIMATIONS

Use Framer Motion for these specific effects:

1. **Hero headline** — staggered word reveal on page load (each word fades up with 0.08s delay between words)
2. **Gate preview cards** — scroll-triggered fade-in stagger as they enter viewport (use `whileInView`)
3. **Feature cards** — scroll-triggered lift-in (y: 20 → 0, opacity: 0 → 1) with stagger
4. **Header CTA button** — subtle teal pulse animation on the border (CSS keyframe, not Framer)
5. **Stats row** (if included) — count-up animation on the numbers when they scroll into view

Do NOT add animations to every element — only the five listed above. Restraint is key.
