Common questions

Before you start

Is the free account really free forever — or does it expire?

Yes, genuinely free forever. The free account gives you the gate overview and regulations hub with no expiry and no payment method required. We don't run free trials that flip to paid — the free tier is a separate, permanent account type.

What happens if I cancel Pro — do I lose my data?

No. If you cancel Pro, your account reverts to the free tier. Your property profile, gate status, and vault documents remain intact. You lose access to the calendar, alerts, and active tracking — but nothing is deleted. If you resubscribe, everything is exactly as you left it.

My property is in Gianyar, not Badung — does DSCVR still apply?

Yes. DSCVR covers all regencies in Bali — Badung, Gianyar, Denpasar, Tabanan, Karangasem, and others. Some local tax rates and permit specifics differ by regency (PB1 for example is set locally). When you map your property you specify your regency, and DSCVR applies the correct rates and requirements. Regulatory alerts are also region-filtered.

I have a property manager who handles compliance — is DSCVR still useful?

Very useful, actually — DSCVR is designed for this scenario. You add your property manager as a staff member and route relevant obligations directly to them via WhatsApp or email. You get confirmation notifications when they mark tasks complete and upload receipts. You maintain oversight without needing to be the one doing the work. This is how most Pro users operate.

DSCVR says I'm missing documents — does that mean I'm non-compliant?

Not necessarily. DSCVR tracks what you've uploaded to the vault, and flags gaps based on that. You may well have a document that simply hasn't been uploaded yet. The flag means "DSCVR can't verify this" — not that the document doesn't exist. Upload what you have and the flag clears. DSCVR is a tracking platform, not an enforcement authority — it doesn't know about documents that exist outside the vault.

Will DSCVR file things or deal with government portals on my behalf?

No. DSCVR is a compliance information and tracking platform — we tell you what needs to be done, when, how, and on which portal. We don't file on your behalf, access your government accounts, or interact with any portal in your name. All filings are done by you or your accountant, using the portal links and plain-language guides DSCVR provides. We keep the manual burden low — but the actual submission stays with you.
